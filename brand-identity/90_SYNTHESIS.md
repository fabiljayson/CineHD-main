## 1. Brand essence (one line)

**Le streaming pensé pour le public francophone — ouvert comme un projet,
exigeant comme un cinéma, payé à sa juste valeur.**

*EN: Streaming built for the French-speaking public — open like a project,
serious as cinema, priced at its true worth.*

## 3. Positioning one-pager

- **For:** Cameroonian viewers who watch on their phones and want it in French.
- **Who are** tired of thin FR catalogs, late dubs, and watching alone.
- **CineHD is** a licensed French-first streaming subscription with WatchParty
  built in.
- **That** unlike Netflix/Canal+ (incomplete FR catalogs, no native
  group-watching) and free sites (malware, 480p, pop-unders), it delivers the
  VF/VOSTFR catalog, real co-watching, and mobile-money pricing from 2 000
  FCFA/month.
- **Unlike** pirate sites, it is licensed, safe to pay with, and open to audit.
- **Category:** SVOD, sub-niche *francophone-first + social viewing*.
- **Trend:** streaming fatigue + francophone neglect + matured mobile-money
  rails.
- **Differentiators (max 4):** ① VF/VOSTFR depth as the premise ② native
  WatchParty (own Rust/WASM engine) ③ mobile-money-native tiers ④ open code +
  premium craft together.

## 5. Aaker brand system

- **Functional benefits:** licensed VF/VOSTFR catalog · WatchParty engine ·
  2 000 FCFA mobile-money entry · auditable open client.
- **Emotional benefits:** pride (a local product at world-class craft) ·
  relief (safe, legal, no malware hunt) · belonging (watching together).
- **Self-expressive benefits:** "I choose the smart, premium, francophone
  option" — taste and identity statement.
- **Brand-as-person:** the cinephile friend who always knows what to watch,
  refuses cam-rips, and built the room you're sitting in.

## 6. Personality & voice (quick rules)

- « **tu** » everywhere except legal/transactional (« vous » for CGV,
  receipts, refunds).
- Short sentences, real film vocabulary, specific numbers over adjectives.
- Honest about limits — the open-code ethos made verbal.
- Dry wit allowed; punch-down never; no superlatives without proof; no
  piracy-adjacent lexicon (« illimité », « tous les films », « torrent »);
  no emoji in product UI.
- Tone shifts by moment (hero: cinematic-minimal · paywall: inviting ·
  payment error: calm-concrete · outage: candid · social: wry).

## 7. Audience snapshot

Primary ICP: « Alex », 18–34, Douala/Yaoundé, phone-first, pays Orange
Money/MTN MoMo, binge series+anime in VF/VOSTFR, discovers titles via
WhatsApp/TikTok, data-conscious. Segments in order: **A** mobile
binge-watchers → **B** social connectors (WatchParty inviters = growth loop)
→ **C** cinephile desktop/extension users. Anti-ICP: never-pays free-site
loyalists, non-CM markets at launch, anyone seeking unlicensed content.

## 8. Narrative spine

**Trueline:** « On l'a construit en public, et on le rend enfin digne de ce
public. »
**Chapters:** ① La communauté (Movix → CineHD, ouvert, anti-monétisation) →
② La bascule (licence MIT, catalogue sous licence, le service devient payant
pour rester debout — dit sans détour) → ③ Le Cameroun d'abord (2 000 FCFA,
mobile money, produit conçu ici).
**Pivot handling:** a founder letter, not marketing copy. Never pretend the
old README didn't exist.

## 9. Non-negotiables (from M10 refusals + design law)

1. **No unlicensed content, ever** — hard line, not a phase.
2. **No ads at launch** — subscriptions only.
3. **Open code stays open** (MIT; transparency is the trust transfer).
4. **Design system is law:** true-black flat, gold **only on data**, white
   inverted-pill primaries (see `.taste-skill/DIRECTION.md` — the visual
   authority document).
5. **No feature-parity chasing** with Netflix — catalog focus + WatchParty +
   price win.
6. **Focus is sacred:** Cameroon only until expansion criteria are met
   (e.g., ≥ 500 CM subscribers before XOF entry).
