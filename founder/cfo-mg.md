# Unit economics: CineHD Cameroon — scenario C: licensed catalog on a flat 1 500 000 XAF/month minimum guarantee

Every number below comes from the input file. Nothing is looked up or guessed.

## One subscriber-month

| line | per subscriber-month |
| --- | ---: |
| Price | $2,000.00 |
| CinetPay collect (2.8% + 100 XAF on 2 000) | -$156.00 |
| CinetPay withdrawal to mobile money (2%) | -$40.00 |
| CDN delivery, ~10 GB/sub/month at ~$0.015/GB (estimate) | -$100.00 |
| **Contribution** (what each subscriber-month leaves to pay the fixed costs) | **$1,704.00** (85%) |

## The margin that matters

Fixed costs: $2,060,000 a month (Infra: app, DB, Redis, DRM, analytics, backups $120,000, Dev + ops, part-time $200,000, Customer support (WhatsApp / phone) $60,000, Legal, accounting, compliance $60,000, Marketing baseline $120,000, Catalog minimum guarantee (estimate — replace with a signed quote) $1,500,000).

- **Break-even: 1,209 subscriber-months a day.** Below that you lose money every month.
- **Profit margin at your plan** (545 a day): **-104%** of every sale, after every cost.
- Capacity: 30000 a day.
