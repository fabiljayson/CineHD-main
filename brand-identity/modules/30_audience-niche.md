## Synthesis

### ICP (primary) — one sentence

**« Alex » — 18–34, Douala/Yaoundé, phones-first, prépaire ou jeune actif,
paie en Orange Money/MTN MoMo, binge series + anime en VF/VOSTFR le soir, partage
tout (liens, extraits, sessions WatchParty) dans ses groupes WhatsApp.**

Behavioural markers: watches 4–6 nights/week · has 0–1 paid sub (price
sensitive) · used free sites, distrusts them · data-conscious (schedules
downloads / evening streaming) · discovers titles via WhatsApp/Instagram/TikTok
clips, not via platform homepages.

### Segment rank (who we take first → later)

1. **A — Mobile binge-watchers (series/anime), 18–34, CM urban** — highest
   intent, pays mobile money natively, retention driver = catalog freshness.
2. **B — Social watchers / friend-group connectors** — the ones who *start*
   WatchParties; multiplies accounts per session (invite loop = growth).
3. **C — Cinephile connoisseurs (desktop + extension users)** — smaller,
   higher tolerance for depth; price less sensitive; keep via quality +
   extension perks.

### Anti-ICP (explicitly not chased)

- Never-pays free-site loyalists (convert later via segment A social proof,
  not pricing).
- Non-CM francophone markets at launch (post-proof expansion only — brief).
- Users seeking unlicensed content — out of scope permanently (M10 refusal).

### Jobs to be done

- "Let me watch tonight's episode in French, on my phone, without hunting for
  a safe link."
- "Let me watch this with my friends even when we're in three different
  quartiers."
- "Let me pay with the money app I already use, small amounts, no card."
- "Let me trust that paying here is safe and legal."

### Open questions / threads (need real data, not assumptions)

- Series vs anime vs film split in CM (drives catalog + homepage editorial).
- Typical data-plan size and evening bandwidth (drives bitrate/lite-mode
  decisions).
- Willingness-to-pay calibration against Canal+/Netflix mobile local prices —
  part of the competitive benchmark (M20).
