## Raw

### Core belief (why does this exist?)

- Founder, session 2026-10-24: chose the commercial path explicitly —
  *« this app is for commercial use actually »* — with three non-negotiables
  recorded that day: **licensed/own catalog only**, **Cameroon first**,
  **subscriptions, no ads**.
- README: *« CineHD (anciennement Movix) est un monorepo produit pour une
  plateforme de streaming communautaire. »* — community platform, not a
  content shop.
- Help copy (`help.openSource.whyBody`): *« Transparence d'abord : tu peux
  vérifier ce que ton navigateur exécute et que le serveur stocke… beaucoup de
  plateformes streaming sont fermées et opaques — ici tout est auditable. »*
- Effort signal: WatchParty was rebuilt from scratch through a Rust/WASM sync
  engine (`wasm/watchparty-sync/`) — watching-together was considered worth a
  native engine, not a widget.
- Craft signal: the 2026-10 redesign pushed the UI to award-grade editorial
  (true-black flat, gold reserved for data) — a francophone product refusing
  the "good-enough for our market" tier.

### The behavioural How (values in action, not poster slogans)

- **Build the whole stack** — web, Android WebView app, Chrome/Firefox
  extension, userscript, WASM sync, proxies: evidence across the monorepo.
- **Trust by auditability** — open code (MIT), BIP39 seed handled client-side
  (*« le serveur ne connaît jamais ta seed »*).
- **Quality as respect** — the redesign's restraint (no moiré, no stock
  colors, gold only where data lives) is the value applied daily.
- **Ship decisions hard** — session choices were fast and binary
  (*"ONLY CAMEROON"*, *"Browse + trailers only"*): focus over hedging.

### What the brand refuses to be or do

- Not a pirate box: **no unlicensed sources, ever** (brief anti-goal).
- Not an ad-infested free site: **no ad tier** at launch.
- Not a Netflix clone: no feature-parity chase, no originals, no live sports.
- Not opaque: silence and black boxes are refusals, not styles.
- *(Historic)* the old README banned monetization outright — superseded
  2026-10-24, but it records the founder's original community-purity instinct;
  what the brand refuses today is **dark patterns**, not revenue.

### Founder quotes strong enough to become internal anchors

- *« ONLY CAMEROON »* — focus as a value (session, 2026-10-24).
- *« No unlicensed sources, ever »* — the hard line (session → brief §6).

   peut naire d'ici et tenir commercialement, sans pub ni compromis. »
