## Synthesis

### Dunford positioning statement

- **For** Cameroonian viewers who watch films and series on their phones and
  want them in French,
- **Who are** tired of thin French catalogs, late or missing dubs, and having
  to watch alone or screen-share to share the moment,
- **CineHD is** a licensed French-first streaming subscription with WatchParty
  built in,
- **That** unlike Netflix/Canal+ (French catalogs incomplete, no native
  group-watching) and free sites (malware, 480p, pop-unders), gives the full
  VF/VOSTFR catalog, real co-watching, and mobile-money pricing from 2 000
  XAF/month,
- **Unlike** pirate sites, it is licensed, safe to pay, and open to audit.

### Category & alternatives

- **Category:** subscription VOD (SVOD), sub-niche = *francophone-first +
  social viewing*.
- **Relevant alternatives viewers actually weigh:** Netflix (mobile plan),
  Canal+ (strong in francophone Africa), Showmax, YouTube, free streaming
  sites, downloading via Telegram/WhatsApp groups.
- **Competitive set for formal benchmarking:** → run
  `competitive-platform-analysis` before marketing copy freezes (verify current
  CM pricing of Netflix/Canal+/Showmax — do not quote unverified numbers).

### Differentiators (max 4, evidence-backed)

1. **VF/VOSTFR catalog depth as the product** (French isn't a dub track, it's
   the premise).
2. **WatchParty native** — own Rust/WASM engine, not a browser trick.
3. **Mobile-money-native pricing** — 2 000 XAF entry, prepaid tiers, no card
   required.
4. **Open + premium** — MIT code anyone can audit, award-grade UI; the only
   service where transparency and craft coexist.

### Trend (why this works now)

Streaming fatigue + price hikes; francophone catalogs remain an
afterthought for globals; mobile-money recurring billing now viable in XAF.
