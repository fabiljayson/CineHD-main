## Raw

- **Design evidence** (DIRECTION.md, shipped 2026-10): true-black flat ground
  (#000/#111), gold #C89028 *only on data* (ratings, ranks, progress), white
  inverted-pill primary buttons, editorial display headings
  (clamp(1.55–2.25rem)/500), no gradients except sanctioned scrims — restraint
  is the craft signature.
- **Wordmark evidence** (README): « triangle doré, CINE argent et HD or sur
  fond noir » — the logo itself is quiet luxury: silver + gold, never loud.
- **Copy evidence** (help pages, FR): « tu » register, candid, slightly wry —
  *« si tu veux comprendre où CineHD stocke, comment ça marche, ou pourquoi le
  serveur ne connaît jamais ta seed »*; EN equally direct (*« we can talk it
  through before you spend three days on it »*).
- **Behaviour evidence**: built a native Rust/WASM WatchParty engine, whole
  stack (web/extension/userscript/RN/WASM) — artisan, not assembler.
- **Constraint evidence**: 2 000 XAF entry price + award-grade UI in the same
  product — premium made reachable.

## Synthesis

### Archetype (primary / secondary)

- **Primary: The Creator** — cinema as craft; the platform is *made*, not
  assembled; editorial, restrained, image-literate.
- **Secondary: The Everyman** — accessible, « tu », priced for the real
  audience; never gatekeeps taste or wallet.
- **Shadow (used sparingly): The Sage** — open code, auditable, "no black box"
  transparency; proves rather than preaches.

### Aaker dimensions (1–5)

| Dimension | Score | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sincerity | 5 | open code, no dark patterns, honest pricing |
| Excitement | 3 | cinema thrill, WatchParty energy — but no hype |
| Competence | 5 | full-stack build, premium UI, fail-closed security |
| Sophistication | 4 | true-black editorial, gold restraint, wordmark |
| Ruggedness | 2 | not outdoorsy/rough — this is a dark cinema, not a campfire |

### Personality in three traits

1. **Cinephile artisan** — obsesses over the frame, the type, the timing.
2. **Candid companion** — speaks « tu », admits limits, explains the seed.
3. **Quietly confident** — never shouts "best/most"; shows quality instead.

### Candidate personas (for reference, not mascot)

- « L'ami cinéphile exigeant » — the friend who always knows what to watch
  and refuses cam-rips.
- « L'artisan du cinéma » — the one who builds the room you watch in.

### Open questions / threads

- How loud may marketing be? Personality says quiet — launch campaigns may
  push tension between hype and restraint (resolve in M50 voice rules).
- Does the Golden Triangle remain the mascot-grade symbol or does WatchParty
  earn its own visual mark? (feeds design system, not here)

### Contradictions or tensions

- **Premium look × budget price** — the productive tension to *own*: luxury
  is in craft, not in tariff. It is the brand's most differentiating edge in
  CM; never resolve it by downgrading the UI.

### Bridge to Module 50

- Voice must sound like this personality: « tu », short sentences,
  cinema-literate, zero corporate filler, humor dry and never at the user's
  expense.
